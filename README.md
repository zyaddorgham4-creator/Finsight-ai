## 💡 About the Project

**FinSight AI** is a modern personal finance dashboard built to make financial information easier to understand and act on.

The idea is not just to display numbers such as income, expenses, budgets, and savings.

FinSight is designed to add an **AI-powered financial intelligence layer** that can analyze financial data, identify important patterns, provide insights, simulate different scenarios, and help users make better financial decisions.

The project combines:

* Personal finance management
* Data visualization
* Goal tracking
* Budget management
* Transaction management
* AI-powered financial analysis
* Financial forecasting
* Scenario simulation

# ✨ Main Features

The main dashboard gives users a quick overview of their financial situation.

It provides important information such as:

* Total balance
* Income

* Savings

The goal is to make the user's financial situation understandable at a glance.

## 💳 Transactions

* Name
* Description

* Date

# 🤖 FinSight AI

One of the main ideas behind the project is the **FinSight AI Assistant**.

Instead of creating a normal chatbot interface, the AI Assistant is designed as a **Financial Intelligence Center**.

The concept is:

> The AI should understand the user's financial situation and help them analyze, plan, forecast, and simulate financial decisions.

---

## 🧠 AI Financial Analysis

Users can ask financial questions such as:

> "Can I afford a new laptop next month?"

or:

> "Why did my spending increase?"

The AI interface can provide structured responses containing:

* Financial metrics
* AI assessment
* Supporting information
* Recommended actions
* Confidence level

The response changes depending on the user's question.

---

## ⚡ Quick AI Commands

The AI Assistant provides ready-to-use commands such as:

* Analyze my spending
* Forecast my cash flow
* Find saving opportunities
* Check my goals

This makes the AI easier to use without requiring users to know exactly what to ask.

## 🔎 Proactive AI Insights

FinSight AI is not designed to only answer questions.

It can also proactively identify interesting patterns in financial data.

Examples include:

* Dining spending increased
* A goal is approaching its deadline
* Unused recurring subscriptions may be costing money
* A financial goal may be delayed
* A possible saving opportunity has been detected

Each insight can be expanded to understand:

* What happened
* Why it matters
* Its potential impact
* Possible action

---

# 🧪 AI Scenario Lab

One of the most important features of the AI Assistant is the **Scenario Lab**.

Instead of only telling the user what happened, FinSight allows them to explore:

> **What would happen if I changed something?**

Example scenarios include:

* Save more
* Cut dining expenses
* Buy a laptop
* Increase income
* Delay a purchase
* Add a new recurring expense

The system then compares:

**Current Trajectory**

vs.

**AI Projected Trajectory**

and shows the difference using financial projections and charts.

This turns the AI from a simple question-answering interface into a **financial decision simulation tool**.

---

# 🔮 AI Forecast

The AI Assistant also includes financial forecasting.

Users can switch between:

* 30 days
* 60 days
* 90 days

The forecast can display:

* Current balance
* Expected balance
* Upcoming expenses
* Projected savings
* Future financial trajectory

The purpose is to help users understand not only their current situation, but also where their finances could be heading.

---

# 🕘 AI Analysis History

FinSight keeps a history of recent AI analyses.

Examples include:

* Laptop affordability analysis
* Spending anomaly analysis
* Vacation goal forecast
* Monthly saving strategy

This creates the feeling that the AI is part of an ongoing financial assistant rather than a one-time chatbot.

# ⚠️ Current Limitations

The current version is primarily a **frontend project and portfolio demonstration**.

For example:

* AI intent detection
* AI responses
* Forecast calculations
* Scenario projections

are currently implemented as frontend/demo logic.

There is currently no real LLM backend connected to the application.

Financial data is also stored locally using `localStorage`.

# 🎯 Project Goal

The main goal of FinSight AI is to explore how a traditional personal finance dashboard can evolve into an **AI-powered financial decision system**.

Instead of only showing:

> "You spent $500 this month."

FinSight aims to eventually answer:

> "Your spending increased mainly because of dining and recurring expenses. If this pattern continues, your savings goal may be delayed. Here are the possible scenarios and the actions you can take."

That difference is the main idea behind the project.
